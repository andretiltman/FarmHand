Your plants, animals and farm jobs in one list

FarmHand tracks your crops from seed to harvest, reminds you when the chickens need feeding or the dogs need walking, and keeps on top of jobs like the septic tank.

What it does

🌱

Seed to harvest

Follow each crop from sowing to seedling, transplant and harvest, with a growing guide and countdowns for every stage.

💧

Watering reminders

See when every plant was last watered and when it's next due – orange when due, red when overdue.

🐔

Animal care

Feed, walk, groom, ride and clean out – chickens, ducks, dogs, horses, snakes and more. Count eggs as you collect them.

🛠️

Maintenance jobs

Recurring jobs like septic tank enzymes, cleaning gutters or servicing the lawnmower, with a due date for each.

🌰

Seed inventory

Keep track of the seed packets you have on hand. Sowing takes the seeds out of your inventory for you.

📷

Photo log

Photograph your plants and animals as they grow. The newest photo becomes their icon on the list.

📤

Share and sync

Send plants, animals and seeds to another phone, or keep two phones in sync for a garden you look after together.

🏠

Home Assistant

Optionally connect your own Home Assistant and every plant, animal and task appears there as a sensor.

🌍

Your hemisphere

Sowing seasons follow where you are, so October is spring in South Africa and autumn in Europe.

Your data stays on your phone

FarmHand doesn't need an account, doesn't show ads and doesn't track you. Everything you enter is saved on your device. It only leaves your phone when you choose to send a file to someone or connect your own Home Assistant. Read the privacy policy for the details.
