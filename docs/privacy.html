Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

In short:

  • FarmHand has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Everything you enter is stored on your own device. We don't run any servers and we never receive your data.
  • Your data only leaves your device when you choose to send a file to someone or connect your own Home Assistant.

This policy explains how the FarmHand mobile and web app ("FarmHand", "the app", "we", "us") handles information. It applies to the app on Android, iOS and the web.

1. Information we collect

We, the developers of FarmHand, do not collect, receive, store or sell any personal information. FarmHand does not include any analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it does not ask you to sign up or log in.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, FarmHand saves the following on your device only (in the app's private storage, or in your browser's storage when using the web version):

  • The plants, crops, animals, maintenance tasks and seed packets you add, including their names, tags, notes, dates and history (waterings, feedings, eggs collected, jobs done and so on).
  • Photos you take or choose inside the app for your plants, animals and seed packets.
  • Your Home Assistant address and access token, if you connect Home Assistant.
  • A random identifier for your device, made by the app, which is used only to tell two phones' changes apart when syncing. It is not linked to you, your device's hardware or any advertising identifier.

This information never reaches us. It stays on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

3. Permissions

PermissionWhy it's used
CameraTo take photos of your plants, animals and seed packets. Only used when you tap to take a photo.
Photos / mediaTo choose existing photos from your library for your plants, animals and seed packets. Only the photos you pick are used.
FilesTo open a FarmHand transfer or sync file that someone sent you, when you tap Receive → Choose file.
Internet / networkOnly to talk to a Home Assistant server that you connect yourself. FarmHand makes no other network connections.

FarmHand does not use your microphone, location, contacts or calendar. You can turn permissions off at any time in your device's settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

4. When your data leaves your device

FarmHand only shares data when you ask it to:

Sending and syncing files

When you use Send or Sync, FarmHand creates a file containing the items you chose (and photos, if you choose to include them) and opens your device's share menu. You choose where the file goes – for example WhatsApp, email, Bluetooth, Nearby Share or AirDrop. That service and the person you send it to will then have the file, and their own privacy policies apply. We never see these files.

Home Assistant

If you connect Home Assistant, FarmHand sends details of your plants, animals and tasks (names, statuses, dates, tags and similar) directly from your device to the Home Assistant address you enter, so they can appear there as sensors. If you turn on Sync with other phones, a copy of your items is also stored in your Home Assistant so your other phones can read it. Photos are not sent. Home Assistant is software you or your household run and control; we have no access to it. Tapping Disconnect removes FarmHand's sensors and forgets the saved address and token.

5. Children

FarmHand is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. Because we don't collect any personal information, we don't knowingly collect information from children.

6. Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security, such as its screen lock and app sandboxing. Files you send are not encrypted by FarmHand, so only send them to people and services you trust. When connecting Home Assistant, we recommend using an https:// address, especially over the internet.

7. Your choices and rights

Because your information is kept on your device and not by us, you are in full control of it:

  • See and change it – everything is visible and editable in the app.
  • Delete it – delete individual items in the app, or delete everything by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Data you have sent to other people or to Home Assistant must be deleted there.
  • Take it with you – use Send or Sync to make a file of your data.

Depending on where you live, laws such as South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) or the EU/UK GDPR may give you rights over your personal information. As we hold none of your information, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side, but you're welcome to contact us with any questions.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how FarmHand handles information, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. If a change is significant – for example if the app ever started collecting data – we will make it clear in the app or its store listing before it takes effect.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or your privacy, please open an issue on GitHub.
